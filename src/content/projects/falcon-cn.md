Designed and developed in 2002: a support site and community for MicroProse's
Falcon 4.0, the hardcore F-16 combat sim. After meeting Fox, Helios, BlueBlood
and others online, we started the CN101TF "Griffin Squadron" virtual squadron,
and the site became its home base.

> "Designed and developed - 'FalconCN.com' in 2002. The sophisticate jet
> combat simulator: Micro Prose Falcon 4.0's support site and community.
> Developed and coded Content management system (ASP Access) for the website,
> as well as Virtual squadron management system which features member register
> and management, flight reporting and auto scoring functions."
> — my Behance intro for the project

## The site

A homebrew ASP + Access CMS with graphic design done in Photoshop. The
homepage (captured 2003-07-19, titled "FalconCN | CN101TF Griffin Squadron")
navigated: Home Base, News, Bulletin Board, Topics, Downloads, WebLinks,
CN101TF Griffin Squadron, Roster, Join our squadron.

Front-page articles included the FreeFalcon 1.0a release, 《Art of the kill
猎杀艺术》 (translated into Chinese by teammate Eagal566), an ECV56 Condor
"Rescap" video, and a Falcon 4.0 quick-start guide. The counter read 55
articles and 79 downloads. Article sections covered 游戏新闻, 飞行学校
(quick-start guides for new players), 战术讨论, and an F-16资料库; downloads
included real F-16 manuals, training programs (RCS-RWR, TWS and Bullseye
trainers), and tools like Tacedit 2.1, F4Weather 1.1 and the ProAttack weapon
delivery calculator.

The footer: "Falcon4 Chinese Support Center | CN101TF Griffin Squadron —
Copyright 2002 FalconCN.com — Designed by Werther-041".

## The squadron system

The member area ran on the Squadron Member Register System (SMRS) v1.0 alpha:

> "Squadron Member Register System (SMRS) v1.0 alpha — Programed by Werther 041"
> — footer of the member roster page, captured 2003-08-01

It offered member registration and management, login, roster, 飞行任务报告
(flight reports), 分数板 (score board), and 队员审核 (member verification).
The roster held 64 members; my own entry is callsign "werther", serial
CN101-041, registered 2003-04-28. The homepage member roll names BlueBlood,
Fox, Helios, Tom528 and Werther. The BBS ("FalconCN.com BBS") ran forums
including a Falcon 4.0 discussion board with 335 topics and 1,807 replies
(captured 2003-07-11). The squadron coordinated over a TeamSpeak channel and
MSN Messenger / MSN Groups.

## Domains

`www.falconcn.com` and `www.cn101tf.com`. Captures of cn101tf.com from
2004–2007 show the same ASP CMS — the squadron era. Later captures are an
unrelated "Buy WoW Gold" site: the domain changed hands and is not part of
this recovery.

## What didn't survive

Flight-report and score-board *contents* were never captured (the nav proves
they existed; only the roster list page was archived). BBS threads beyond the
forum index, and the member edit/verify flows, are also missing. One loose
end: my 2002 VirtualFly article links to `http://f16-falcon.myrice.com` as
"CN101中队的网站", but every archived capture of that host shows a 3GO
squadron site instead — unresolved, kept as a lead in the archive notes.

`docs/archive-recovery/cn101tf/`.
