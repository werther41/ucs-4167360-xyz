My first website, started in 1999 after playing Jane's IAF and an F-15 flight
sim. Built with MS FrontPage, later rebuilt with Dreamweaver and Fireworks
for graphics. It grew into what my portfolio calls "a Flight simulation
portal and community in China," with news, a large file library, and forums.

> "My first website – 'FlightSim Net' in 1999, and later it became a Flight
> simulation portal and community in China. It provides Flight Simulation's
> premier resource for news and resources throughout the hobby. Extremely
> large file library with thousands of files updated daily. Multiple forums
> covering all aspects of flight simulation and support forums for many of the
> well known providers of add-ons"
> — my Behance intro for the project

## V1 — static HTML on 163.com

The first free domain, `591fly.yeah.net`, redirected to `go2.163.com/~werther/`,
where the V1 site lived as static HTML. The Wayback Machine holds 76 captures
of 62 pages from April to July 2001.

The homepage (captured 2001-06-07) was organized into GAME LOOK (game reviews
and previews), FLYING SCHOOL (keyboard charts, guides, flight manuals),
DOWNLOAD (patches and plugins), FLY STORY, and COOL PLANE (aircraft profiles
and photos). It had a login form with free registration, a friend-links row,
and a "Powered by Bentium" credit. The update notes were written in
first person — in February 2001 I was pleading for forum visitors:

> "论坛怎么没人来呀，这个论坛功能挺强的，一定来看看。"
> ("Why is nobody coming to the forum? This forum is quite powerful, do come
> take a look." — homepage update notes, captured 2001-06-07)

Typical content: an X-Plane trial download (70 MB), an F-15 Strike Eagle guide,
MiG-29 briefings, IAF mission briefings, a KA-52 walkthrough. The footer read
"Copyright © 591fly.yeah.net" with contact "my oicq:4167360".

## V2 — PHPNuke portal on 591fly.cpgl.net

V2 moved to PHPNuke on free PHP + MySQL hosting from cpgl.net. The domain root
served a redirect stub titled "Flight Simulation Net" (captured 2002-05-30);
the portal itself survives in a 2003-02-01 snapshot of
`591fly.cpgl.net:8080/index.php`, titled 飞行游戏网. By then it had 1,500
registered users, with articles posted by "werther" between August and
November 2002 — "F4 Picth 简体中文版", "Falcon4中文手册", "《轰炸中队》(Dam
Busters)发布" — plus sections for articles, downloads, weblinks, a screenshot
gallery, and the 飞网论坛 forums, a Top-10 weblinks chart, and a "飞龄" (years
flying) poll.

One October 2002 article points readers to "CN101中队的网站" at
`http://f16-falcon.myrice.com` — see the note in the archive README about
what that host actually shows.

## What didn't survive

The `virtualfly.net` domain itself is long lost and was not checked. Forum
threads, guestbook entries, and the actual download files were never captured —
only index and section pages. Captures of `/newss/` and `/service/` on this
host from December 2004 are CPGL's own portal content (the free host's CMS),
not VirtualFly's.

Recovered snapshots: `docs/archive-recovery/virtualfly-v1/` and
`docs/archive-recovery/virtualfly-v2/`.
