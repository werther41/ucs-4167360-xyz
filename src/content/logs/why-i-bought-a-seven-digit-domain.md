The site lives at `4167360.xyz`, which is not a word, does not stand for anything, and is not my phone number. People ask, so here is the spreadsheet.

## The short answer

Numeric `.xyz` domains of six digits or more are priced differently from ordinary ones. This one was under a pound a year. I have spent more than that failing to decide on a name.

## The long answer

I had been trying to buy a domain for about four years. Every name I liked was taken, parked, or being held for four figures by someone who had also thought it was a good name. Every name that was available made me wince slightly, and a domain you wince at is a domain you do not write on.

So I changed the question. Instead of looking for a name that meant something, I looked for a string that meant nothing at all and could not be misread over the phone. Seven digits, no ambiguity between letters and numbers, no dashes.

The spreadsheet was me checking availability across a few hundred candidates and scoring them on how they looked in a monospace font, which tells you most of what you need to know about how I make decisions.

## Does it matter

Not really, and that is the point. Nobody types a domain any more. They follow a link or they search for the name of the thing, and the name of this thing is Uncommon Solid, which is at least pronounceable.

The number is just an address. I find I quite like having an address that refuses to explain itself.
